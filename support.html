💬 Need help with HabTrack?
Whether you've run into a bug, have a question about how something works, or just want to share an idea to make HabTrack better, we'd love to hear from you. Reach out any time at boulos.noah@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
🐞 Reporting a bug
To help us fix things faster, try to include: • What you were doing when it happened • What you expected vs. what actually happened • Your device model and iOS version You can also report issues directly from the app via Settings → Support → Report a bug.
🔒 Privacy questions
For anything related to your personal data, check out our Privacy Policy — or just email us, same address as above.
